Shop users

Everyone who can sign in to the app. There is no sign-up page: only a super admin can add a login.

What each role can do

Sales Billing (scan, search, checkout), online orders, and their own sales and bills. Can cancel or fix their own bill for 30 minutes.

Admin Everything sales can do, plus all sales by everyone, cancelling any bill, items & prices, the PDF settings and shop settings.

Super admin Everything, plus this Users tab: add people, set passwords, change roles, switch logins off.
